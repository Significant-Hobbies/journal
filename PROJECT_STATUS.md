# Journal — PROJECT STATUS

Last updated: 2026-08-23

## Why / What

Journal is a private native writing app for morning and evening reflection,
free writing, date navigation, and finding prior entries. It is useful fully
offline and optionally synchronizes through the Significant Hobbies Hub.
